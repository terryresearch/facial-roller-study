Before you begin

You are invited to take part in a short research study about how people evaluate everyday consumer products. Please read this page before deciding whether to take part.

  • What you will do. You will read a short article about a consumer product and answer a series of questions about it. The study takes about 4–6 minutes.
  • Voluntary participation. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this window, without giving a reason and without penalty.
  • Anonymity. Your responses are anonymous. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.
  • Use of the data. Responses are stored securely and analysed only in aggregate for academic research purposes.
  • Risks and benefits. There are no anticipated risks beyond those of ordinary daily life. There is no direct benefit to you other than any compensation stated in the recruitment advertisement.
  • Questions. If you have questions about this study, you may contact the research team using the details provided where you found this study.
